# Frontend Design & UI Excellence for Flutter

This skill guides the creation of distinctive, production-grade Flutter interfaces that avoid generic or boring aesthetics.

## Design Thinking

1. **Aesthetic Direction**:
   - Modern fintech styling: Dark/Light mode support with harmonious slate and deep teal accents, vibrant status badges, and refined typography.
   - Micro-interactions: Smooth page transitions, ripple touches, animated bottom sheet slides, and custom chart animations.
2. **Typography & Readability**:
   - Hierarchical text scaling: Distinct large display for monetary amounts (`TextStyle(fontSize: 28, fontWeight: FontWeight.bold)`).
   - Monospace or tabular figures for transaction amounts to ensure aligned digits.
3. **Visual Depth & Cards**:
   - Subtle borders, soft drop shadows (`BoxShadow(color: Colors.black.withOpacity(0.04), blurRadius: 10, offset: Offset(0, 4))`), and rounded corners (`BorderRadius.circular(16)`).
   - Glassmorphic overlays and backdrop filters for modals and sheets where appropriate.
4. **Color Palette & Data Visualization**:
   - Curated category colors (Emerald for Groceries, Amber for Dining, Indigo for Shopping, Sky Blue for Transport, Purple for Bills).
   - High contrast between text and card backgrounds for outdoor readability.
